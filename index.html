Your first greenhouse is just the beginning.

In Greenhouse Valley, a small beginning gradually becomes your own green retreat. Choose your seeds, care for your plants and gather your harvest. Each new region opens up another piece of the world.

From blooming gardens to quiet lakes, every region brings its own atmosphere and new plants.

01 / PLANT

Small beginnings, green dreams.

Choose what grows in your greenhouses, from your first radishes to new varieties from distant corners of the valley.

02 / NURTURE

A little care goes a long way.

Water your plants, watch them grow and equip your greenhouses with helpful automation.

GOOD TO KNOW

Still curious?

What is Greenhouse Valley?

A game about greenhouses, plants and exploring a connected world. Plant seeds, care for your crops and gradually unlock new regions.

Can I download the game yet?

Greenhouse Valley is in development. There is no public download or confirmed release date yet. You'll find the details here as soon as they're available.

What do the images on this page show?

The hero artwork comes from the game's loading screen. The three region images show gameplay with the interface and greenhouses: the starter garden, the watermill and the sluice. All images are from development and may change.

What is there to discover?

Blooming gardens, lakes, forests, meadows, vineyards, orchards and more. New regions bring new plant varieties, more places for your greenhouses and new gameplay mechanics. Every region you unlock offers new possibilities to explore.
